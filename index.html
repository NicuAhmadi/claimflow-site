From unsent claim to submitted — automatically

ClaimFlow runs a fully automated pipeline against your existing practice management and clearinghouse setup. Your team does nothing.

📥

Fetch unsent claims

Automated

Match every patient

Instant

All patients are resolved against your practice database in a single batch pass — before any claim is processed. No manual lookups, no guesswork, no mis-matches.

Automated

Imaging is exported directly from your imaging system — exam folders matched to each claim's date of service, exported at full resolution. No one opens the imaging software.

What's inside

Purpose-built for dental billing

Not a generic automation tool. Every feature was designed around the actual Eaglesoft → Vyne workflow.

🧠

AI X-ray selection

AI analyzes every candidate image and picks all relevant attachments — pre-op + post-op for crowns, multi-quadrant BWs for perio, full FMX for pre-auths.

📋

Clinical narrative generation

Procedure-specific narratives for billing and pre-auths — crowns, root canals, SRP, and more. Short billing mode and full pre-auth mode.

🔌

Deep practice software integration

Connects directly to your practice management database and imaging system. No data migration, no new software for staff. Works with your existing setup.

🔄

Resume & retry logic

Skips already-completed claims on re-runs. Failed exports retry automatically. Package cache avoids duplicate Claude API calls.

📊

Full audit trail

SQLite audit DB tracks every step. CSV reports after each run. Summary report with failed claims, FMX flags, and retry candidates.
